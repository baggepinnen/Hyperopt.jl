BOHB

BOHB implements the model-based sampling of BOHB: Robust and Efficient Hyperparameter Optimization at Scale. It is used as the inner sampler of Hyperband and replaces the random sampling of the configurations: a fraction ρ of the configurations is drawn at random, and the remaining configurations are proposed by maximizing the ratio $l(x)/g(x)$ of two kernel density estimates, fitted to the best and to the worst configurations evaluated with the largest budget for which sufficiently many observations are available. Before sufficiently many observations are available, all configurations are drawn at random.

Dimension types

The keyword argument dims contains one dimension type per hyperparameter, in the order of the candidate vectors:

  • Continuous() for ordered, real-valued hyperparameters, including integer-valued ones. The densities are estimated in the coordinate given by the index into the sorted candidate vector, scaled to $[0, 1]$. A logarithmically spaced candidate vector, such as exp10.(LinRange(-4, -1, 100)), is thus modeled in a logarithmic coordinate.
  • Categorical(k) or, equivalently, UnorderedCategorical(k) for categorical hyperparameters with k candidates, e.g., Categorical(2) for [false, true].

Every configuration proposed by BOHB consists of elements of the candidate vectors.

Example

The example of the page Hyperband is extended by the categorical hyperparameter nesterov, which selects Nesterov's accelerated gradient instead of gradient descent with momentum:

const scales = [1.0, 10.0, 100.0]
loss(w) = sum(scales .* (w .- 1) .^ 2)
gradient(w) = 2 .* scales .* (w .- 1)

function train(w, v, lr, β, nesterov, epochs)
    for _ in 1:epochs
        g = nesterov ? gradient(w + β * v) : gradient(w)
        v = β * v - lr * g
        w = w + v
    end
    w, v
end

function evaluate(lr, β, nesterov, epochs, state)
    w, v, trained = state === nothing ? (zeros(3), zeros(3), 0) : state
    w, v = train(w, v, lr, β, nesterov, round(Int, epochs) - trained)
    loss(w), (w, v, round(Int, epochs))
end

ho = @hyperopt for epochs = 81,
                   sampler = Hyperband(η = 3, inner = BOHB(dims = [Continuous(), Continuous(), Categorical(2)])),
                   lr = exp10.(LinRange(-4, -1.5, 100)),
                   β = LinRange(0, 0.95, 100),
                   nesterov = [false, true]
    evaluate(lr, β, nesterov, epochs, state)
end
printmin(ho)
lr = 0.013219411484660293
β = 0.6813131313131312
nesterov = false

The configurations proposed by BOHB are evaluated as new configurations, i.e., state is nothing in their first evaluation. The observations of BOHB are the evaluated configurations and their losses; the states returned by the loop body are not used by BOHB. The results are recorded as for any other inner sampler of Hyperband, see Hyperband:

ho.minimum, ho.sampler.minimum.budget
(8.246103158478552e-9, 81.0)

Reproducibility and parameters

All random decisions of BOHB use the random number generator of its random sampler, a run is thus reproducible with, e.g., BOHB(dims = [Continuous(), Continuous()], random_sampler = RandomSampler(Xoshiro(0))). The observations are retained when an optimization is continued with ho = .... See the docstring of BOHB for the remaining parameters, which correspond to the symbols of the paper.
